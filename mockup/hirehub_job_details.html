About the role

We are looking for an experienced Senior Frontend Developer to join our engineering team at Grameenphone. You will be responsible for building and maintaining high-quality web applications that serve millions of users across Bangladesh. You'll work closely with our product and design teams to deliver exceptional digital experiences.

Responsibilities
  • Develop and maintain scalable React/Next.js applications
  • Collaborate with designers and backend engineers
  • Write clean, maintainable TypeScript code with unit tests
  • Lead code reviews and mentor junior developers
  • Optimize application performance and loading speeds
  • Participate in agile ceremonies and sprint planning
Requirements
  • 5+ years of experience in frontend development
  • Strong proficiency in React.js, TypeScript, and Next.js
  • Experience with state management (Redux / Zustand)
  • Solid understanding of web performance optimization
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent
  • Strong communication skills in English & Bengali
