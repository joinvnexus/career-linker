About Grameenphone

Grameenphone is the leading telecommunications service provider in Bangladesh, with over 80 million subscribers. As a subsidiary of Telenor Group, we are committed to connecting people and empowering communities through reliable and innovative digital solutions across Bangladesh.

We foster a diverse, inclusive workplace where employees are encouraged to grow and innovate. Our mission is to empower society with connectivity and digital services.
